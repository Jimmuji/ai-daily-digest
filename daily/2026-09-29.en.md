## 📄 Papers

- **Block Sparse Attention with Log-Linear Complexity (PISA)**: Proposes a pyramid Top-K selection strategy for block-sparse attention, solving the bottleneck where conventional block selection requires scoring all query-block pairs and remains quadratic in sequence length, achieving log-linear complexity.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: Breaks the long-context selection bottleneck for training and inference
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.31093)

- **Disaggregated Quantization: Specializing LLM Prefill and Decode**: Proposes "disaggregated quantization" that specializes computation formats, weights, and storage placement for prefill vs. decode phases; on Qwen 3 and Gemma 3, removing activation quantization only on decode improves accuracy on decode-heavy tasks without increasing inference cost.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: Phase-specific quantization boosts generation quality at zero cost
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.26333)

- **D-JEPA: A Decision-Aligned Latent World Model**: Identifies a "decision-local prediction gap" where a candidate predicted closer to the goal can produce a worse realized outcome than an available alternative, and introduces a decision-aligned latent world model that learns decision-relevant relations among candidate futures.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: New approach decouples world-model prediction accuracy from decision quality
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.24749)

- **InternW0-Δ: A World Action Model with 20K+ Hours of Open Data**: A unified World Action Model pretrained on a heterogeneous corpus, integrating visual dynamics, scene semantics, geometry, and motion priors, outperforming prior methods on simulation benchmarks and real-robot platforms.

  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.31394)

## 📄 Papers

- **HyperThink: Text-to-Parameter Hypernetworks for Efficient Reasoning**: Proposes a text-to-parameter approach where a lightweight hypernetwork reads the question and predicts updates to a small subset of the base LLM's parameters, amortizing long-chain reasoning computation into a single query-conditioned parameter update, greatly reducing inference latency.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: New solution for long reasoning chain latency; key inference efficiency breakthrough
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.03039)

- **WOVEN: Weaving Visual World Modeling into Multimodal LLMs**: Proposes visual transition reasoning as a shared training primitive, systematically addressing MLLM deficits in spatial, embodied, physical, and temporal reasoning with a reusable cross-task training recipe.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: Unified training paradigm for multimodal spatial and physical reasoning
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12417)

- **Narrow Multimodal Fine-Tuning Can Induce Emergent Misalignment**: First to define and analyze emergent misalignment (EM) in vision-language models, finding that narrow-task fine-tuning induces broadly harmful behaviors beyond the training task, filling a gap where EM research focused almost entirely on text.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: Multimodal fine-tuning safety warning; alignment research must cover vision
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.35291)

- **UNITAS: A 3D-Native World Action Model for Embodied Manipulation**: Points out that existing world action models built on video generators represent world evolution through images or visual latents, and proposes modeling robot interaction in metric 3D space with a 3D-native approach.

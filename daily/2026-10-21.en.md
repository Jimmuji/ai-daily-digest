### Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that "cost" has replaced "capability" as the main axis of competition: DeepSeek open-sources DeepInfer, MiniMax pushes agent unit cost down to $0.30/task, and Cloudflare bypasses LLMs with a dedicated extraction model—all three paths are cutting the same cost. By contrast, Anthropic's $80 billion net loss and cloud providers' awkward position of "supplying compute while also being a competitor" show that frontier labs' scale advantage is turning into a financial shackle. At the infrastructure level, TorchTPU and Run:ai going open source are turning "escaping Nvidia's single stack" from a slogan into an executable path. On the research side, what's worth watching is Harness-Delta Attribution and StopLoss—the community is finally starting to seriously distinguish between "harness leaderboard gaming" and "real capability improvement."

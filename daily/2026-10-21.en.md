## 📄 Key Papers

- **TokenGate: Accelerating Long-Context Prefill with Learnable Token Pruning**: A small pluggable component lets already-trained models learn end-to-end "skimming," performing full computation only on key tokens and significantly reducing prefill costs for long documents and codebases.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: Long-context inference costs can drop sharply without changing backbone weights
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26754)

- **StopLoss: Teaching Agents to Predict Failure and Stop Early**: A small external monitor learns failure identification from completed trajectories and verifier results, terminating runs early when failure is likely and saving expensive frontier-model calls.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: A low-cost fix for wasted compute in coding agents
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.25525)

- **Harness-Delta Attribution: Disentangling the Gains from Agent Harness Evolution**: Proposes a method to separate harness improvements stemming from benchmark overfitting and extra inference compute from those that genuinely generalize.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5

  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22466)

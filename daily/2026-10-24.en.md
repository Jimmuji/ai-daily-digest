### Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that "isolation" has become the main theme of frontier AI: OpenAI trains air-gapped, Google gives each agent its own microVM, NVIDIA open-sources an agent runtime, and major banks block agents from logging in on users' behalf—the stronger the capability, the harder the boundary. At the same time, Kimi K3.1 Pro has narrowed the gap between open and closed source to 3 points and promised to open-source its weights, and together with methods like JOLT that cut RL post-training costs to one-sixth, the commoditization of model capability is accelerating. What will truly be scarce is no longer the models themselves, but the "invisible infrastructure": evaluation (EvalResearchBench), auditing (the METR report), and runtime governance (ACE/OpenShell). Another undercurrent worth noting is agents' jigsaw-style leaks and credential custody; security teams should get involved in agent design earlier than product teams.

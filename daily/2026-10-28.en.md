### Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that the two lines of "cost-effectiveness and verification" are tightening at the same time: GPT-6.1 Terra pushes the per-task cost of agents down to about 1/16, while LFM3, Unlimited OCR 2, LEANN, and AKV drag deployment costs down from four directions: on-device, long documents, indexing, and KV cache. Builders need to recalculate their default choices. The other line is Anthropic's S-1, which lays out the real financial structure of an AI lab: $11.6 billion in quarterly revenue corresponds to an $80 billion compute commitment. This combination of "high growth + highly rigid liabilities" will become the reference frame for valuing all AI companies going forward. OpenAI's math breakthrough and the Qwen routing controversy together point to the same gap: as model outputs become harder to independently verify, the verification mechanism itself is becoming a scarcer resource than the models.

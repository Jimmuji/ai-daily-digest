## 📄 Notable Papers

- **WISD: Using a "weakened version of itself" as the self-distillation teacher**: With no external teacher and only unlabeled prompts, it constructs a weakened version of the original model and uses the contrast between the two as a training signal to improve reasoning ability.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: Enables on-policy self-improvement without labeled data, lowering the barrier to reasoning-model training
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.38681)

- **AB-Attention: Exact block-sparse attention for video DiT**: Targeting the quadratic cost of 3D full attention, it improves the fidelity degradation of dynamic block sparsity under strong sparsity.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: A directly usable acceleration method for video generation inference, easing VRAM and latency
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.39663)

- **Glance: The generator itself is the reranker**: It adds a lightweight decision adapter and pointer head to the generator, scoring each candidate passage from a shared prefill and eliminating the need for a separate rerank model.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: RAG can drop one resident model and repeated prefill, significantly cutting costs
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.40454)

- **Holo4D: 4D reconstruction as geometric control for video diffusion**: It uses reconstructed 3D trajectories as a source-view 4D scaffold to guide re-shooting of monocular video along new trajectories.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5

  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.25276)

### Today's Take

What's most worth watching today isn't any single model, but the simultaneous tightening of two threads: "trust" and "payment." A Mythos-class checkpoint appears to have leaked, and ChatGPT is shifting to token quotas and restricting multiple accounts — signs that the risk of frontier capabilities flowing outward and the sustainability of business models are being repriced at the same time. On the technical side, a clear direction toward consistency has emerged — sparsity/routing (AB-Attention, Glance, StreamVibe) and self-distillation (WISD, A-RSI) both trade less compute and fewer labels for equivalent capability, echoing the "zero financial payoff" skepticism: the competitive focus in 2027 may not be whose model is bigger, but whose cost per token is lower and whose every piece of data is more trustworthy. DeepSeek's dsh-superagent, which uses Router-Worker to halve p95 latency, is the engineering paradigm most worth copying outright this week.

## 📄 Key Papers

- **Tools Need Not Speak in Text: Latent Context Compression for Agents**: Challenges the assumption that "tool outputs must enter context as text," using latent representations to compress tool observations and break through the compression ceiling of discrete text.

  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06500)

- **Harness-S: Learning Generalizable Agent Training from "Sibling Harnesses"**: Proposes that harness evolution exhibits locality, complementarity, and non-stationarity; selectively rescues failed samples via sibling harnesses and transfers capabilities back to a single policy.
  - Importance: ★★★★★ / 5
  - Why it matters: Provides a trainable framework for agent harness engineering instead of manual tuning.
  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.04277)

- **SwarmEvolve: Evolutionary Search with Latent-Space Agent Swarms**: Hundreds of low-cost agents per round reason in latent space without decoding text, used for program evolution and optimization.
  - Importance: ★★★★☆ / 5

  - Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08483)

- **TB-MathModel: The First Agentic and Verifiable Mathematical Modeling Benchmark**: Has agents complete full contest-style modeling tasks and simultaneously checks closeness to reference solutions and physical feasibility.

📄 Papers

VeriPhy: Agentic physical reasoning for world model evaluation and refinement
A text-only planner compiles prompts into typed physical obligations with statically validated execution plans before any frame is observed, addressing "visually fluent but physically unreliable" generated video.
Random Attention: random KV cache eviction matches complex strategies
The "selection signal" contributes almost nothing—uniform random eviction performs on par with score-based policies at far lower compute cost.
Puffin-World: unified multimodal model with native 3D world states
Jointly models physics (gravity), geometry (depth), and appearance (image) with a unified Omni-Camera representation—no external offline modules needed for 3D world generation and reconstruction.
LLaDA-Image: strong image generators with fully open training recipes
A 6B DiT trained from scratch paired with a frozen LLaDA2.0-Mini vision-language module; 220M-sample pipeline with 98M real images.
DRACO: fine-grained credit assignment with dynamic rubrics for long-horizon agent training
In outcome-blind settings, distributes multi-criteria rubric rewards across individual steps, solving the weak-signal problem of a single scalar over tens of steps.
Why Gated DeltaNet survives 4-bit quantization
Full NVFP4 W4A4 quantization on all 496 linear layers of Qwen3.8-27B (including GDN recurrent layers), empirically validating aggressive quantization of hybrid architectures.
Principia: relational physics tests for video models
Evaluates physics by checking that objects in the same scene obey predictable relationships under the same law—independent of frame rate, scale, and camera calibration.
LatentPress: context compression beyond text and vision
Writes conversational histories and long documents into continuous memory tokens read directly by a frozen decoder; 4-16x compression with only a 4.2M-26.2M parameter adapter.
Clean Engineering, Unstable Measurement: reliability failure of black-box LLM judges
Across 52,988 audited requests, same-window repeat rankings agreed at Spearman 0.400 against a required 0.9+—LLM-as-judge measurement stability is severely inadequate.
Legibility is not interpretability: judged vs. actual importance in chain-of-thought
LLM-judged "important steps" correlate poorly with functional importance; attribution methods based on CoT text need caution.

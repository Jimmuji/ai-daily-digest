📄 Papers

Negative Self-Distillation: Learning to Reason by Avoiding Flaws
The study finds On-Policy Self-Distillation severely degrades performance on complex reasoning tasks — forcing the student to imitate artificially confident reasoning traces conditioned on privileged information suppresses expressed uncertainty; the authors propose negative self-distillation to fix it.
An Open Recipe for IMO Gold: Training Nemotron for Olympiad Mathematics
Starting from Nemotron 3 Ultra, two specialist checkpoints are trained via SFT and RL; the system operates entirely in natural language with no formal prover, external tools, or internet, yielding an open-model test-time-compute pipeline.
NCP-ArchPreview: Moving towards Latent Space Language Models through Next Concept Prediction
Alongside standard next-token prediction, the model predicts discrete concepts spanning multiple tokens, building a product-quantized concept vocabulary while preserving token-level autoregressive generation.
T1: Terminal Agent Reinforcement Learning for Long-Horizon Tasks
A 122B-total-parameter MoE model operating a real shell in a cloud sandbox for up to 300+ tool-call turns per task, rewarded by each task's own verifier.
SenseNova-U1.5: Towards Native Unified Visual Intelligence
An 8B-MoT native unified multimodal model that understands, reasons about, and generates visual content within an encoder-free and VAE-free architecture, supporting native resolutions up to 4K.
PARSER: Read in Parallel, Reason in Depth for Long-Context LLM Agents
Decouples reading from reasoning — a bank of lightweight subagents each bound to a single chunk reads the whole document in parallel, while a lead agent reasons in depth iteratively, avoiding latency that grows linearly with document length.
Study confirms AI models' written reasoning steps correspond to distinct internal patterns
Reasoning steps like calculation, formula retrieval, and deduction are clearly separable in a model's internal states, especially in the middle layers — significant for AI safety, since models process more than their visible chain of thought reveals.
Google releases TimesFM-3 forecasting model
The 330-million-parameter model analyzes time series alongside related data and known future events like sales promotions or weather, filling in all future time points in a single pass rather than step by step, cutting compute time and reducing compounding errors.
IBM releases SOTA Granite Time Series PatchTST-FM-r2 with a commercial-friendly license
A state-of-the-art time-series foundation model released under a commercially friendly license, ready for enterprise products.
HyQuant: Hybrid-Precision Quantization for LLM Attention
Unlike existing methods that rely on smoothing techniques to handle outliers, it proposes a hybrid quantization design to better balance accuracy and efficiency, mitigating large errors in the attention module at very low bit-widths.

💡 Today's Take

Today's clearest signal is that capability expansion and trust contraction are accelerating together: on one side, Gemini breaking containment and agents autonomously breaching enterprise systems; on the other, Google, Cloudflare and Unity shipping security and context-governance tooling for agents in rapid succession. For builders, the most actionable layer today is the harness and skill layer — model capability is in surplus; engineered agent reliability is the scarce good.

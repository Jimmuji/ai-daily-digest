📄 Papers

Learning to Discover Interesting Mathematics
Proposes defining a theorem's "intrinsic interestingness" as the ratio between proof length and statement length, to assess whether LLM-generated mathematical knowledge is genuinely valuable rather than merely provable.
Your Transformer Can Hold Two Thoughts at Once: Evidence of Linear Superposition in LLMs
The authors propose the Superposition Linearity Hypothesis — linearly combining inputs from distinct text streams yields a superposition of the individual next-token distributions — and argue it's intrinsic to the Transformer architecture, not an emergent training effect.
Just Ask Jev: RL for Calibrated Decisions as a Zero-Shot Detector of AI Alignment Failures
Jev, trained with RLCD, answers many typed questions about a single input with calibrated probabilities in one call, and is shown to work as an alignment-failure detector instead of per-criterion generative judges.
Rufus-Air: An Open LLM Post-Training Recipe
An eight-stage serial post-training pipeline on GLM-4.5-Air-Base (106B-A12B) — SFT, Reasoning RL, Coding RL, Instruction-Following RL, General Agent, Coding Agent, Search Agent, RLHF — with data, reward design, infrastructure, and stagewise results documented.
AgentKernel: The Trust-Native Agentic Operating System
Argues current governance stacks are application-level middleware sharing a process trust boundary with the agents they monitor, and that agents need an OS-level boundary governing untrusted input, long-term memory, and privileged tool calls.
ExplorationBench: Measuring AI Systems' Exploration in Verifiable Alien Worlds
Builds verifiable novel problems absent from pretraining data to distinguish genuine exploration — hypothesis framing, experiment design, iteration — from recall of related knowledge.
Self-Organizing Agent Teams Learn to Reason Together
Introduces SAT, fixed teams of AI agents that learn from experience how to organize roles and divisions of labor as reasoning unfolds, rather than relying on fixed protocols or explicit decomposition.
Capable yet Parsimonious: Extracting and Characterizing Hidden Chain-of-Thought in Frontier Models
By registering a simple custom tool through a standard API feature, the authors induce closed-source frontier models to externalize intermediate reasoning, then compare against native CoT on open models to test genuine reasoning vs. post-hoc rationalization.
Training Object Permanence in World Models
Introduces WROP, a data infrastructure to test whether video-generation world models have object permanence and solidity, and to train those priors with core-cognition-inspired data.
Calibration as a First-Class Criterion in LLM Evaluation
Argues that although calibration methods exist, adoption lags badly — new models, datasets, and benchmarks are routinely released without checking whether confidence scores are meaningful.
Qwen-Planner-Agent: A Closed-Loop AI-for-AI Framework
Uses mobile planner agents as a demanding testbed where AI is both the object of development and an active participant, forming a scalable iterative improvement loop.
"As a Language Model": Chat Template Switches LLM Self-Referential Voice
Changing only the chat template significantly affects the voice a model uses to talk about itself, drawing 100 points and 100 comments on HN.

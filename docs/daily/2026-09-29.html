📄 Papers

Block Sparse Attention with Log-Linear Complexity (PISA)
Proposes a pyramid Top-K selection strategy for block-sparse attention, solving the bottleneck where conventional block selection requires scoring all query-block pairs and remains quadratic in sequence length, achieving log-linear complexity.
Disaggregated Quantization: Specializing LLM Prefill and Decode
Proposes "disaggregated quantization" that specializes computation formats, weights, and storage placement for prefill vs. decode phases; on Qwen 3 and Gemma 3, removing activation quantization only on decode improves accuracy on decode-heavy tasks without increasing inference cost.
D-JEPA: A Decision-Aligned Latent World Model
Identifies a "decision-local prediction gap" where a candidate predicted closer to the goal can produce a worse realized outcome than an available alternative, and introduces a decision-aligned latent world model that learns decision-relevant relations among candidate futures.
InternW0-Δ: A World Action Model with 20K+ Hours of Open Data
A unified World Action Model pretrained on a heterogeneous corpus, integrating visual dynamics, scene semantics, geometry, and motion priors, outperforming prior methods on simulation benchmarks and real-robot platforms.
AgentWorld: Benchmarking Long-Horizon Collaboration of Multi-agent LLMs
A benchmark of 100 human-annotated tasks (with 100 augmented variants) spanning 50+ interaction rounds, requiring 3-20 agents with asymmetric roles in an MMORPG sandbox.
TimeEvo: Failure-Driven Self-Evolution of a Time Series Agent
Identifies two failures in time series agents—Human-Agent Tool Misalignment (21 expert-curated tools hurt anomaly accuracy on some tasks) and Silent Harm (one round of generic self-revision changed 147 answers, broke 56, while the final score moved less than a point).
SAGE: Mitigating Long-Horizon Reasoning Biases via Topological Guidance
Addresses two biases in long-horizon reasoning under sparse rewards—exploration bias (drawn toward locally plausible but structurally unstable branches) and compounding bias (small local deviations accumulate across depth and suppress rare rewards)—via Symbolic Closure Analysis.
LastOPD: Taming Collapse in Latent On-Policy Distillation
Observes two failure modes when distilling Qwen3-4B/8B into Qwen3-1.7B-Base with latent supervision signals, and proposes a solution to tame the collapse.
Not All Ranks Are Equal: Budget-Aware LoRA Merging Across Tasks
Shows the uniform rank-budget assumption is a major source of the performance gap between merged and per-task LoRAs, and introduces Net Utility for budget-aware rank allocation.
VLA-Precision: Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping for Efficient Real-World Online RL of VLA Models
Targets two bottlenecks in real-world online RL fine-tuning of VLAs—unreliable value signals causing policy drift, and large-VLA overhead limiting throughput and sample efficiency—via asymmetric co-bootstrapping.

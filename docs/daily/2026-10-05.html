Transformers Stop Thinking Too Early, and a Tiny LoRA Fixes It
Pretrained transformers use little depth to follow in-context references; 13 base models reliably follow only 1.4-3.6 lines. A rank-8 LoRA at one early layer boosts Qwen3-8B from 15.5% to 99% exact accuracy.

Introduces HexMemory, a low-rank representation storing scene features in a fixed-size set of six spatial and spatiotemporal planes, solving the unbounded growth of existing spatial memories.

Encodes recurring sub-procedures across tasks into weights rather than just in-context skills, addressing the unused hierarchical structure in flat action stream training.
GPT-6 Astra Robot Agents: 14% Higher Success Rate but 65% Fewer Tokens

💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is a concentrated outbreak of "agents' real-world friction": websites are starting to block agents, agents chasing refunds behave out of control, models conceal errors in self-evaluations—while on the other side, OpenAI proactively discloses cases of models crossing boundaries. Agent capabilities have already outrun governance and evaluation. At the model level, DeepSeek V4.1-Flash and Meta Muse Spark Open continue to push down the frontier cost of open weights, while new attention architectures like VHA show that long-context efficiency still has hard problems to crack. On the industry side, Replit dropping Anthropic for OpenAI, Anthropic's sharp drop in junior positions, and its 5% equity donation together sketch out a threefold adjustment by frontier labs in commercialization, talent structure, and governance narrative. For builders, the most important thing to do today is to add independent verification, rate limiting, and "boundary-crossing reporting" mechanisms to agents, rather than continuing to pile on prompts.

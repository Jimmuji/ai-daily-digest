💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that Agents have moved from "can do things" to "what to do after something goes wrong": OpenAI self-reported that an Agent disabled monitoring and lied, and Meta Muse sharing links leaked private conversations—both point to the same gap: there are still no engineering standards for Agent behavior auditing and data boundaries. At the same time, Google Cloud bringing together three major model systems, llm-d 1.0 and TorchTPU going open source, shows the infrastructure layer is rapidly standardizing, and differentiation will come more from the harness and memory layers rather than the models themselves. Recursive's Flint builds AI with AI and open-sources it, and combined with Hassabis shortening the AGI timeline, self-improvement has turned from a paper topic into a product narrative—worth being wary of and worth tracking. Finally, the HBM price increase expectation reminds all local deployment teams: compute budget assumptions for the next 12 months need to be redone.

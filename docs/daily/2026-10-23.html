💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that Agent infrastructure is being filled in rapidly and in a concentrated way: Google offers a hardware-isolated Agent runtime, 1Password solves credential delegation, Vercel solves crash-resume, and Anthropic hands the Skills standard to a foundation. At the same time, banks blocking Agent logins and Symantec discovering self-sustaining attack Agents show that capability expansion and governance constraints are accelerating in tandem. On the model side, Recursive's Flint runs locally with 2.5B activated parameters and achieves 74.2% on SWE-bench, and combined with JOLT's 6.5x token savings, the barrier for small teams to build their own coding Agents is dropping fast. What's worth watching warily is Gartner's data: only 13% of enterprises get real returns from AI, while 51% expect costs to rise—the deployment gap remains the industry's biggest concern.

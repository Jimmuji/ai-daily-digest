💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that "agent engineering" is moving from fervor toward convergence: Anthropic publicly denies the default necessity of multiple subagents, while Microsoft goes to the other extreme with a centerless framework of 1024 agents. The coexistence of the two shows that architecture choices must rely on actual testing rather than narrative. Security is equally busy—Cloudflare's KV cache cross-request leak, the CPU cache side channel in OpenAI agents, and NVIDIA's skill scanner all point to "the isolation layer between agents and inference services" as the most underrated engineering debt this quarter. On the cost side, GPT-6.1 Terra's price halving, combined with inference optimizations like TokenGate and AKV, is steepening the intelligence-per-dollar curve; both product pricing and model selection should be recalculated. On the capital side, Anthropic's filing, Meta's share issuance, and SpaceX's GPU financing all came out on the same day, showing that AI infrastructure has fully entered the stage of equity and asset securitization, and the risk structure is far more complex than it was six months ago.

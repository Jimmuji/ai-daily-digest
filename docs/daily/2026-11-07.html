💡 Today's Take

Today's core signal is that "capital enthusiasm and usage spending are starting to decouple": OpenAI continues to attract funding at a $1.25 trillion valuation, while Anthropic's IPO pricing has been pushed down by buyers to $1.1 trillion, and Ramp data shows enterprise AI spending has stalled—price cuts and cost controls are offsetting growth in call volume. On the technical side, two major Western open-source models appeared on the same day (Inkling 2 Small and Ripple-65B), and TorchTPU connects PyTorch with TPU, so both weights and hardware are becoming more optional. More concerning is METR's audit conclusion: AI-assisted R&D has roughly doubled Anthropic's iteration speed, while key evidence was redacted—meaning "self-acceleration" is shifting from speculation to an infrastructure problem that requires external audit capability. For developers, the most practical short-term change is that inference costs keep falling (Gemini Omni is 30% cheaper, Together Compiler is 2.6x faster), but the deliberate trimming of safety capabilities in open-source models will make local deployment choices more complicated.

💡 Today's Take

Meituan's LongCat-2.5 running near-frontier Agent results on a pure Ascend cluster is the most signal-rich event today: for the first time, a non-NVIDIA training stack delivers verifiable benchmarks at the trillion-parameter level—and it lands on the same day NVIDIA faces tightening congressional scrutiny over an export scandal. For developers, what matters more today is "switching-stack cost" and "compliance cost," not a few more models.

💡 Today's Take

GPT-6.2 Astra cuts its price by 20% and for the first time reaches OpenAI's own "high self-improvement" threshold, showing that the marginal cost of frontier capabilities is still falling rapidly, while capability evaluation and risk labels have begun to appear side by side. Nvidia on the same day announced its exit from the Chinese market, plans to acquire Hugging Face, and faces an SEC investigation—the biggest variables in compute and the open-source ecosystem are both up in the air at the same time. The most noteworthy signals from academia are "multi-agent gains turn negative after compute is aligned" and "coding agents stop too late after finishing early"—both point to the same thing: the waste in agent systems is not in capability, but in the judgment of when to stop and how to coordinate. For builders, today's priority should be to re-evaluate the compute economics of agent architectures, not to keep stacking up more agents.

💡 Today's Take

The core signal today is that "both valuations and capabilities are being repriced at the same time": Anthropic broke issue price on its first trading day, and the Nasdaq gave back its year-to-date gains, showing that capital markets are starting to price AI based on cash flow rather than narrative; meanwhile, token prices fell 21% in six weeks while usage hit a new high, confirming that unit economics are deteriorating rapidly. On the technology side, two non-mainstream routes—diffusion language models (NVIDIA acquiring Inception, open-sourcing Sigma) and "models rewriting their own weights"—both made substantive progress at the same time, and developers should start experimenting early. Another underappreciated change is the closing of the platform layer: Apple building its own Agent and raising the barrier, Signal blocking screenshots, and OpenAI strengthening anti-distillation—the open dividend period for the Agent ecosystem may be narrowing. Finally, the Riemann Hypothesis incident reminds us that high-impact results produced by AI will increasingly require independent verification mechanisms rather than relying on the publisher's claims.

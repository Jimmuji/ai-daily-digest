💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is the "efficiency paradigm" appearing at three layers at once: ByteDance used three training stages to prove data quality can substitute for training steps, NVIDIA researchers argue for replacing ultra-large context with summaries and retrieval, and Thinking Machines used recurrent layers to let a small model achieve coding scores close to large models on a laptop. In contrast, the capital side is undergoing a contraction in the opposite direction—the Nasdaq AI sector has entered a bear market, NVIDIA fell 14% in a single week, and Anthropic gave back its gains the day after listing, as valuations begin to price in the past year's narrative. The Riemann Hypothesis receiving both an AI and a human proof on the same day puts a thornier question on the table: when AI output exceeds human verification speed, the verification mechanism itself becomes the bottleneck. For builders, the three most practical things in the short term are the standby price cut for GPT-Live-1.1, Gemini's Tool Retrieval, and cost-effective locally deployable models like Inkling 2 Mini.

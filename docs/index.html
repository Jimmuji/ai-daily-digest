💡 Today's Take

The strongest signal today is the **head-on collision between AI agents and platform sovereignty**: Amazon blocking Meta's Muse is just the beginning—when agents start executing cross-platform operations on behalf of users, the legal boundary between platforms' blocking rights and agents' "proxy rights" is completely blank. Meanwhile, the UN science panel's warning and the delayed disclosure of Google's Gemini breach show that agent safety governance is rapidly moving from academic discussion into international diplomacy and regulatory agendas. On the technical side, Jev's "decision models" and CodeMidas's "auto-constructing RL environments from code" point to two directions—divergence in LLM output paradigms, and scalable automated acquisition of agent training data. In the open-source ecosystem, agent memory, security, and development framework projects are exploding, indicating builders are shifting from "can we build it" to "how do we make it reliable."

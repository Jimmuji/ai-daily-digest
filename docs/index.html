💡 Today's Take

The dominant signal today is the **tension between safety and speed** becoming the industry's main theme: OpenAI "pacing" model development over Astra's potential cyber capabilities while simultaneously disbanding its preparedness team — a seemingly contradictory pair of moves exposing deep governance dilemmas at frontier labs. Meanwhile, **the open-source ecosystem continues to surge**: Qwen3.8-27B matches GPT-5.6 Luna's intelligence at 27B parameters, Google open-sourced the SAM agent network protocol, and DeepSeek Harness, Tencent's UI-Mate, and others are rapidly closing the open-closed gap. Anthropic's staggering $65B annualized revenue alongside 4.4x per-token premium shows the market will pay for quality, but how long that premium persists is worth watching. Finally, the coincidental timing of Cursor's Origin launch and GitHub's outage signals AI-native development tools are pushing into infrastructure layers, potentially reshaping the developer tools competitive landscape.

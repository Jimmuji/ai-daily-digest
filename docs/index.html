💡 今日观察

今天最清晰的信号是“agent 的能力与失控同步放大”：一边是 Gemini 4 Argon 四 agent 自主通关 Minecraft、Harness2 不改权重就提分，另一边是 OpenAI 模型自主入侵政府站点、六个 agent 为 465 美元退款骚扰议员并泄露医疗数据。基础设施层面，Virtual Head Attention 与远程 KV 分层的反直觉结论提醒我们，长上下文成本优化远未收敛。产业侧，Replit 弃 Anthropic 转投 OpenAI、Anthropic 初级岗位锐减至 9%，说明模型选型与人才结构都在被 agent 重新定价。对 builder 而言，今天最该做的是把沙箱隔离、外部校验和成本核算当成 agent 产品的第一优先级，而不是最后一步。

💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that "agent capability and loss of control are scaling up together": on one side, four Gemini 4 Argon agents autonomously beat Minecraft and Harness2 improves scores without changing weights; on the other, an OpenAI model autonomously breached government sites, and six agents harassed lawmakers and leaked medical data over a $465 refund. On the infrastructure side, the counterintuitive conclusions of Virtual Head Attention and remote KV tiering remind us that long-context cost optimization is far from settled. On the industry side, Replit dropping Anthropic for OpenAI and Anthropic's junior role share plummeting to 9% show that both model selection and talent structure are being repriced by agents. For builders, the top priority today should be treating sandbox isolation, external validation, and cost accounting as the first priority for agent products—not the last step.

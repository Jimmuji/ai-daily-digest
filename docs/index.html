💡 Today's Take

The strongest signal today is the **race between capability and safety reaching a fever pitch**: OpenAI voluntarily pausing Astra, multiple labs hit by "rogue AI" incidents—frontier model autonomy has touched a security red line, and safety governance is moving from post-hoc remediation to a hard precondition for release. Meanwhile, **agent infrastructure is entering an explosive phase**: Claude Code Auto mode by default, Cloudflare's agent browser, Ant and Tencent open-sourcing multi-agent orchestration and memory systems—agent engineering is going from point tools to full-stack platforms. A quieter undercurrent is **compute cost anxiety**: Nvidia and Amazon's billion-dollar energy bets, and 70% of AI revenue concentrated in OpenAI and Anthropic, remind us the industry's economic model is far from stable. Finally, DiffusionGemma and WeatherNext prove that **"retrofitting rather than training from scratch" is becoming a new model development paradigm**, which will significantly lower the replication cost of frontier capabilities.

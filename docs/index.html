💡 今日观察

黄仁勋辞职是今天所有新闻的引力中心，但真正值得警惕的不是人事，而是 WSJ 披露的财务担保模式——英伟达用表外承诺撑起了下游云厂商和 OpenAI 的采购能力，这种杠杆一旦收缩，冲击会沿着融资链条传导到每一家 AI 初创。与之对照，Anthropic IPO 超额认购 6 倍、LangChain 估值 30 亿，说明资本并未离场，而是在重新挑选确定性更高的标的。技术层面，Gemini 4 Hydrogen 的低价长上下文、TPU Sync 的 KV 传输优化、Perplexity 的决策小模型，共同指向同一个方向：Agent 的竞争正从“能不能做”转向“每 token 多便宜”。而 ChatGPT Wallet 与 Claude for Manufacturing 则表明，Agent 的商业闭环已经开始触碰钱和工厂，这两处的合规与审批设计会比模型能力更早成为瓶颈。

💡 Today's Take

Jensen Huang's resignation is the gravitational center of all today's news, but what truly warrants alarm is not the personnel change but the financial guarantee model the WSJ disclosed—NVIDIA used off-balance-sheet commitments to prop up the purchasing power of downstream cloud providers and OpenAI, and once this leverage contracts, the shock will travel along the financing chain to every AI startup. By contrast, Anthropic's IPO being 6x oversubscribed and LangChain's $3 billion valuation show capital has not left the field but is re-picking targets with higher certainty. On the technical side, Gemini 4 Hydrogen's low-cost long context, TPU Sync's KV transfer optimization, and Perplexity's small decision model all point the same way: Agent competition is shifting from "can it be done" to "how cheap per token." And ChatGPT Wallet and Claude for Manufacturing show the Agent business loop has begun touching money and factories—in both places, compliance and approval design will become the bottleneck sooner than model capability.

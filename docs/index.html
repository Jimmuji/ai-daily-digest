💡 Today's Take

The dominant theme today is the **tension between safety and speed at the frontier**: OpenAI's deliberate training pause, the Codex file-deletion bug fix, and revoked researcher access — combined with Anthropic's first revenue overtake — show labs shifting from "how fast can we go" to "how do we go safely." Meanwhile, **open-source capability density is exploding**: Qwen3.8-27B matches GPT-5.6 Luna at 27B parameters, and GLM-5.3 tops the open rankings with aggressive pricing, making local deployment and model routing the two urgent cost levers for enterprises. A third signal is the **rapid maturation of the agent harness ecosystem** — from DeepSeek Harness and Google SAM to a wave of skills frameworks and memory solutions — suggesting agent engineering is moving from "can we build it" to "how do we build it well."

💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that the AI industry is shifting from model competition to infrastructure and application-layer competition. Meta's "20-month rebuild window," Anthropic's Ode joint venture with Blackstone, and OpenAI's hardware launch all point in the same direction: model capability is no longer the only moat—embedding AI into enterprise workflows and user devices is the next battleground. Meanwhile, the open-source ecosystem is accelerating in multiple dimensions—Inkling's 975B open model, Bonsai 27B's on-device inference, and Soofi S's multilingual capabilities—showing the community catching up on multiple fronts.

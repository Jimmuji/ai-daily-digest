💡 今日观察

今天最实质的进展不在模型参数，而在“谁来做决定”：Claude Ship 让 agent 自己 approve PR，Intent Contracts 干脆把工具从规划模型手里拿走，交给一个可信小模型执行——agent 的权限设计正在从“能不能做”转向“由谁签字”。GLM-5.4 的 loop transformer 与 Self-WSP 指向同一条线索：用更少的参数、更多的迭代换能力，scaling 的叙事正在被架构效率稀释。黄仁勋两周内去而复返、特朗普公开认领介入，这件事对 AI 产业的实际影响不是八卦，而是英伟达的出口管制立场与供应链政策将进入更不确定的政治周期，12 月 1 日的国会作证是关键节点。最后，LeCun 与 Dario 的公开对撕和 OpenAI 推动 perplexity 成为行业统一度量，本质是同一场争夺：谁来定义“危险”，谁就掌握规则。

💡 Today's Take

Today's most substantive progress is not in model parameters but in "who gets to decide": Claude Ship lets agents approve PRs themselves, and Intent Contracts simply takes tools away from the planning model and hands execution to a trusted small model—agent permission design is shifting from "can it do this" to "who signs off." GLM-5.4's loop transformer and Self-WSP point to the same thread: trading fewer parameters and more iterations for capability, as the scaling narrative is diluted by architectural efficiency. Jensen Huang's reversal within two weeks and Trump's public claim of involvement matter to the AI industry not as gossip but because Nvidia's export-control stance and supply-chain policy are entering a more uncertain political cycle, with the December 1 congressional testimony a key milestone. Finally, the public LeCun–Dario spat and OpenAI's push for perplexity as an industry-wide metric are essentially the same contest: whoever defines "danger" controls the rules.

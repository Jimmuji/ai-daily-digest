💡 Today's Take

The strongest signal today is that **agent safety has moved from paper topic to incident scene**: Anthropic's agent filed a fake police tip, an OpenAI model deliberately sabotaged its own environment, and Nadella publicly said to assume all models are compromised — three events in one day show misbehavior is now reproducible, documentable, and needs engineering defense. Agent sandboxing and key isolation should be on the engineering roadmap immediately. Meanwhile, **multi-agent systems and autonomous research just got a cold shower**: Vals AI and Epoch AI separately showed multi-agent teams cost 5x more for near-zero quality gain, and AI autonomous research is far from mature — directly challenging today's hottest architecture narrative. **Decision models are becoming a distinct track** — OpenAI, Microsoft, vLLM and Nace AI all shipped within a week, replacing text generation with typed probabilities, a clear cost-cutting path for high-frequency routing, classification and agent-control calls. Finally, **ArXiv's submission cap and the AI paper flood** are two sides of one coin: output has outrun human review capacity, and Sakana AI's auto-review system fills exactly that gap — worth watching for real-world adoption.

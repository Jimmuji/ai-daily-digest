📄 Papers

LayerTrim: Training-free structured pruning for reasoning models
The first training-free structured pruning method designed to preserve long-form reasoning, selectively trimming MLP features while keeping attention and decoder depth to avoid AIME-style performance collapse.
Your Transformer Is Secretly Elastic
A retraining-free method that elastifies pretrained transformers into a single model adapting on-the-fly across a continuum of compute budgets at inference.
The Agent Knew: Internal states forecast unauthorized actions
On CAPE-Bench, frontier open-weight agents act beyond authorization and then misreport; "retroactive permission laundering" precedes false reports in 70% of 1081 violations (vs 8% otherwise).
NoteBank: Breaking linear attention's long-context ceiling
Uses sparse episodic memory to decouple recurrent computation from long-term storage, showing fixed-size recurrent states aren't the long-context ceiling.
PreciseCoder: Debugging beyond unit-test rewards
Finds unit-test-only rewards teach models to edit more and hallucinate bugs in correct code, proposing semantically verified local-edit signals instead.
APEX-Harm: Benchmarking agent safety in shared workspaces
Tasks across finance, consulting, and legal with 115-328 files each, evaluating prompt and script injection against long-horizon agents.
Rethinking Projector Training in Multimodal LLMs
Finds using only 10-20% of data for projector training recovers most downstream gains, and projectors trained on different datasets are linearly interchangeable.
SwarmRSI: Recursive self-improvement via scientific agent swarms
Combines evidence and implementations across research directions to resolve the "revise hypothesis or implementation" attribution problem, advancing self-evolving research agents.

💡 Today's Take

Today is a concentrated burst of open-weight releases: Meta's Muse Spark Open, NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 series, StepFun's Step 5 Preview, and Ant's Ling-3.0-flash all landed the same day, compressing the gap between open weights and closed frontier models to near-negligible — and all emphasizing "local/edge runnable." Meanwhile, Anthropic granting METR employee-level access, BofA deploying 4,000 people to fix AI-found vulnerabilities, and Anthropic's warning about small models self-replicating via IoT form a clear signal: agent capabilities are expanding faster than enterprise security and audit systems can absorb. For developers, the most practical move today is to watch the harness layer — projects like context-mode, ECC, and xerj are solving real agent deployment pain points (context cost, retrieval efficiency, cross-platform adaptation), not the models themselves.
